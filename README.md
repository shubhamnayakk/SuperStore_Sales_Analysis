## 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis & Forecast Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Superstore sales performance and generate 15-day sales forecasts.
This project delivers key business insights across regions, categories, and customer segments while enabling data-driven decision-making.

---

## Key Features
- 📌 Sales, Profit & Quantity KPI tracking
- 📈 Month-on-Month Sales & Profit Analysis
- 🌍 Region & State-wise Sales Performance
- 🛍️ Category & Sub-Category Insights
- 💳 Payment Mode & Shipping Analysis
- 🔮 15-Day Sales Forecasting using historical trends

---

## Dashboard Highlights
- Total Sales: 1.6M+
- Total Profit: 175K+
- Total Quantity: 22K+
- Average Shipping Days: 4

---

## 📁 Dataset
- Superstore dataset (sample business dataset)
- Includes: Orders, Customers, Products, Shipping & Geography data

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI – Data Visualization & Dashboarding
- Power Query – Data Cleaning & Transformation
- DAX – Measures & Calculations

---

## 📌 Insights Derived
1. West region contributes the highest sales (~ 33%)  
2. Consumer segment dominates (~ 48% share)  
3. COD is the most preferred payment mode
4. Technology & Office Supplies lead in category sales
5. Sales show seasonal spikes and growth trends over time

---

## 🔮 Forecasting
- Implemented time-series forecasting for the next 15 days  
- Helps in demand planning and inventory decisions

---

## 💡 Use Case
This project demonstrates how businesses can:
- Monitor performance in real-time
- Identify high-performing regions/products
- Forecast future sales trends
- Make data-driven strategic decisions
